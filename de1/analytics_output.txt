4. Analytics Outputs

A. hourly_grid_summary

Grain:

one row = one grid_id + one hourly timestamp

B. daily_grid_summary

Grain:

one row = one grid_id + one day

Potential fields:

grid_id
date
daily_total_activity
daily_avg_activity
daily_max_activity
daily_min_activity


C. hotspots

Identifies grids showing unusually high activity relative to their expected baseline.

Potential fields:

grid_id
timestamp
total_activity
baseline_activity
activity_ratio
hotspot_score
hotspot_flag

Important:

A hotspot means unusually high activity, not confirmed network congestion.

We don't have network utilization, throughput, latency, packet loss, etc., so we must not call it a confirmed congestion event.


D. activity_alerts

Contains detected activity anomalies.

Potential fields:

alert_id
grid_id
timestamp
activity
baseline
ratio
alert_level
created_at

E. risk_scores

This is the ML/derived risk output.

Potential fields:

grid_id
timestamp
risk_score
risk_level
model_version
scored_at
